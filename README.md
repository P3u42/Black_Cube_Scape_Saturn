🎮 About the Game

The Black Cube of Saturn is a 3D obstacle-dodging game inspired by the first project of GameDev.tv's Unity 3D course, Obstacle Dodge.

You play as the Black Cube of Saturn, an ancient machine god embodying order, logic, and absolute control. Despite its divine nature, the entity has become trapped and must navigate a dangerous environment to escape.

What appears to be a simple black cube is actually something much greater: a god-machine challenging the very system that was designed to contain it.

🕹️ Gameplay

Navigate a 3D environment as the Black Cube.

Dodge obstacles and avoid deadly hazards.

Test your timing, movement, and reflexes.

Survive as you progress through the obstacle course.
